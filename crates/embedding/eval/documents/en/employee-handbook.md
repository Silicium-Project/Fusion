# Employee Handbook

Fictional document, written to measure the quality of the Silicium search.

## 1. Working hours

Core hours are 10 am to 4 pm, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees organise their day freely, as long as they work 37.5 hours per week on average.

## 2. Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave per year, including public holidays. Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Up to five unused days can be carried over to the next year; they must be taken before the end of March.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees who are ill must inform their manager before 10 am on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required from the fourth consecutive day. Full salary is paid for the first 20 days of sick leave in a calendar year.

## 4. Parental leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers to 4 weeks. Parental leave can start up to two weeks before the expected date of birth or adoption.

## 5. Remote work

Employees may work from home up to three days per week, after agreement with their team. A one-off home-office allowance of 400 pounds is paid to buy a desk, a chair or a screen. Working from another country for more than 30 days per year requires approval from HR.

## 6. Expenses

Business travel is booked through the travel tool. Train travel is preferred for journeys under four hours. Meal expenses during business trips are reimbursed up to 40 pounds per day, with receipts. Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days.

## 7. Equipment and security

Each employee receives a laptop, which must be encrypted and locked when unattended. Passwords are stored in the company password manager, and two-factor authentication is mandatory for all work accounts. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT team within 24 hours.

## 8. Training

Each employee has a training budget of 1,000 pounds per year and two days of paid training time, to be agreed with their manager.

## 9. Leaving the company

Employees who resign must give one month's notice during their first year, and two months' notice after that. Equipment must be returned on the last working day.
