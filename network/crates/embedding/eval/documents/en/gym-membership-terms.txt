These terms apply to all memberships of PulseHaus Fitness clubs operated by PulseHaus Leisure Ltd in Leeds, York and Harrogate, and they form a binding agreement between the club and the member from the date of signature. This is a fictional document written to measure the quality of the Silicium search. By joining, the member confirms that they are at least 16 years old; members aged 16 or 17 need the written consent of a parent or guardian and may not use the free weights area without an induction.

Three membership plans are available. The Flex plan costs 39.90 pounds per month with no minimum term and gives access to one home club. The Commit plan costs 29.90 pounds per month with a minimum term of twelve months and also gives access to a single home club. The Unlimited plan costs 49.90 pounds per month with a minimum term of six months and gives access to all clubs, unlimited group classes and the sauna. A one-off joining fee of 25 pounds applies to all plans and is waived for students holding a valid NUS card.

Monthly fees are collected by direct debit on the first working day of each month. If a direct debit fails, the club will try to collect the payment again after seven days and will charge an administration fee of 10 pounds. If two consecutive payments fail, access to the club is suspended until the arrears have been paid in full. Fees may be increased once per calendar year with at least 30 days' written notice; members on the Commit plan are protected from any increase during their first twelve months.

Members may freeze their membership for medical reasons, pregnancy, or travel abroad. A freeze lasts between one and three months and can be requested twice in any rolling twelve-month period. During a freeze a reduced fee of 5 pounds per month is charged, except for medical freezes supported by a doctor's note, which are free of charge. The minimum term is extended by the length of the freeze. Freeze requests must be submitted at reception or in the app at least five days before the next collection date.

Flex members may cancel at any time by giving one full calendar month's notice. Commit and Unlimited members may cancel after the end of their minimum term with one full calendar month's notice. Notice must be given in writing, by e-mail to members@pulsehaus.example or through the app; verbal cancellation at reception is not accepted. A member who moves more than 15 miles away from every PulseHaus club, or who suffers a long-term injury preventing exercise, may terminate the minimum term early by presenting proof of address or a medical certificate.

Each member may bring guests to their home club. Flex and Commit members receive two free guest passes per calendar year, while Unlimited members receive one free guest pass per month, which cannot be carried over. Additional guest passes cost 8 pounds per visit. Guests must be at least 18 years old, must be accompanied by the member throughout the visit and must complete a health questionnaire at reception. The same guest may visit no more than four times in any twelve-month period.

Group classes can be booked through the app up to seven days in advance. Members who do not attend a booked class and do not cancel it at least two hours before the start receive a no-show strike; three strikes within 30 days lead to a booking ban of seven days. Personal training sessions are not included in any plan and cost 42 pounds for a single 60-minute session or 380 pounds for a block of ten sessions valid for four months.

The clubs are open from 6:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and from 8:00 to 20:00 on weekends and bank holidays. The clubs are closed on 25 December and 1 January. The club may close individual areas for maintenance with 48 hours' notice, and no refund is given for closures of fewer than seven consecutive days. Lockers are for use during the visit only; items left in lockers after closing time are removed and kept at reception for 14 days.

Members must scan their personal membership card or the QR code in the app at the entrance and must not share access with anyone. A replacement card costs 5 pounds. Letting another person in on your membership card leads to an immediate suspension of 30 days and, in the case of a second offence, termination of the membership without refund.

Complaints should first be raised with the club manager. If the matter is not resolved within 14 days, the member can write to the PulseHaus customer care team at 4 Wellington Yard, Leeds LS1 4DX. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
