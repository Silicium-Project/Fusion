# Privacy Policy

Fictional document, written to measure the quality of the Silicium search.

## Who we are

This policy explains how Example Bikes Ltd collects and uses the personal data of the users of its rental application.

## Data we collect

We collect your name, email address and phone number when you create an account, your payment details when you rent a bike, and the location of the bike during a rental, to find it and to compute the price. We do not track your location outside of a rental.

## Why we use your data

We use your data to provide the rental service, to send you receipts, to prevent fraud and to improve the application. With your consent only, we send you news and offers by email; you can withdraw your consent at any time from your account settings.

## Retention

Account data is kept as long as your account is active and deleted two years after your last rental. Trip locations are kept for 13 months. Invoices are kept for ten years, as required by accounting law.

## Sharing

Payment data is processed by our payment provider and never stored on our servers. We do not sell your personal data. We share data with the police only when legally required.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and object to its use for marketing. Send your request to our data protection officer at privacy@example-bikes.test; we answer within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

## Cookies

The website uses strictly necessary cookies for login, and audience-measurement cookies only if you accept them in the cookie banner.
