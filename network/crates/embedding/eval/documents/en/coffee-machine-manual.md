# Barista One coffee machine — user manual

Fictional product, written to measure the quality of the Silicium search.

## Safety

Always place the machine on a flat, dry surface, away from the edge of the worktop. Never immerse the machine, the power cord or the plug in water. Unplug the machine before cleaning it and when it is not used for a long time.

## First use

Rinse the water tank and fill it with fresh cold water up to the MAX mark. Press the power button: the light flashes while the machine heats up, which takes about 30 seconds. Run two cups of hot water without coffee to rinse the circuit.

## Making coffee

Insert a capsule, close the lever and press the small cup button for an espresso (40 ml) or the large cup button for a lungo (110 ml). The cup volumes can be programmed by holding the button down until the desired volume is reached.

## Cleaning

Empty the capsule container and the drip tray every day. Wash them with warm water and mild soap; they are not dishwasher safe.

## Descaling

Descale the machine every three months, or after 300 coffees, using the descaling solution supplied. Hard water makes descaling necessary more often. Fill the tank with the solution and half a litre of water, place a large container under the outlet and hold both cup buttons for five seconds to start the cycle, which lasts about 20 minutes. Rinse the tank and run a full tank of clean water afterwards.

## Troubleshooting

If the light blinks orange quickly, the machine needs descaling. Error E3 means the water tank is empty or badly positioned: refill it and push it firmly into place. If no coffee comes out, check that the capsule was pierced and that the lever is fully closed. If the coffee is not hot enough, preheat the cup with hot water.

## Warranty

The machine is covered by a two-year warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty does not cover damage caused by scale build-up, the use of non-compatible capsules, or a fall.
