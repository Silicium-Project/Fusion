# Crema Pro 2 espresso machine — owner's guide

Fictional product (model CP-2200, manufactured by Vellmar Appliances), written to measure the quality of the Silicium search.

## Safety instructions

Install the Crema Pro on a stable, heat-resistant surface with at least 10 cm of free space behind the appliance for ventilation. Connect it only to an earthed socket rated 230 V / 10 A; do not use extension leads or multi-plug adapters. The steam wand and the group head reach temperatures above 120 °C during use, so never touch them with bare hands. Children under 12 must not operate the machine, even under supervision.

## What is in the box

The package contains the machine, a 58 mm stainless-steel portafilter, a single basket (9 g) and a double basket (18 g), a plastic tamper, a 350 ml milk jug, two Aquaclean filter cartridges and one bottle of Vellmar DS-50 descaler. Keep the original packaging for at least the duration of the warranty: returns are only accepted in it.

## Water tank and filter

The removable water tank holds 2.1 litres and is located at the back of the machine. Before the first use, soak an Aquaclean cartridge in cold water for five minutes, then screw it into the socket at the bottom of the tank until it clicks. Replace the cartridge every eight weeks or after 50 litres of water, whichever comes first. The filter indicator on the display turns blue when a new cartridge is due.

## First start-up

Fill the tank with fresh water, switch on the main switch on the right side panel and wait for the boiler to heat up. The heat-up time is about 45 seconds; the ring around the dial pulses white while heating and stays steady when ready. On the very first start-up the machine runs an automatic priming cycle of 250 ml through the group head and the steam wand. Discard this water.

## Preparing an espresso

Grind 16 to 18 g of coffee for a double shot and distribute it evenly in the double basket. Tamp with a firm, level pressure of roughly 15 kg. Lock the portafilter into the group head by turning it from left to right. Turn the dial to the single-cup symbol for a 30 ml ristretto-style espresso or to the double-cup symbol for 60 ml. Extraction should take between 25 and 30 seconds; if it runs faster, grind finer.

The brewing temperature can be set to 90, 92 or 94 °C. Hold the dial pressed for three seconds to enter the settings menu, turn it to choose the temperature and press once to confirm. The factory setting is 93 °C, which is shown as "STD".

## Frothing milk

Fill the jug one third with cold milk. Turn the dial to the steam symbol and wait about 20 seconds until the steam light stops flashing. Purge the wand for two seconds, immerse its tip just below the surface and open the steam valve fully. Stop when the jug becomes too hot to hold, around 65 °C. Wipe the wand with a damp cloth immediately after each use and purge it again to clear any milk residue.

## Daily and weekly cleaning

After each session, rinse the portafilter and run a short flush of water through the group head. Empty the drip tray when the red float rises; it holds 600 ml. The drip tray, the baskets and the milk jug are dishwasher safe; the water tank is not.

Once a week, backflush the group head: insert the blind disc into the basket, add half a teaspoon of Vellmar GC-10 cleaning powder, lock the portafilter and select the "CLEAN" program in the settings menu. The program alternates five pumping cycles of ten seconds with pauses and lasts about four minutes.

## Descaling

The Crema Pro tracks water consumption and requests descaling automatically: the word "CALC" appears on the display after 80 litres with the filter installed, or after 40 litres without a filter. In practice, this corresponds to descaling roughly every six weeks for a household drinking four coffees a day.

To descale, remove the Aquaclean cartridge, pour 250 ml of DS-50 descaler into the tank and top up with water to the 1-litre mark. Place a container of at least 1.5 litres under both the group head and the steam wand. Open the settings menu and choose "DESC". The cycle runs in two phases: 18 minutes of descaling, then a pause during which the display asks you to rinse and refill the tank with clean water, followed by a 7-minute rinse. Never interrupt the cycle by switching off the machine; if power is lost, the cycle restarts from the beginning.

## Error codes

Code F1 means the water tank is missing or empty; fill it to the MAX line and slide it in until it stops.
Code F2 indicates that the boiler temperature sensor is faulty; switch the machine off and contact customer service.
Code F4 appears when the pump runs for more than 60 seconds without flow, usually because the grind is far too fine or the group head is blocked.
Code F7 signals that a descaling cycle was interrupted and must be completed before the machine can be used.
Code F9 means overheating; let the machine cool down for 30 minutes with the main switch off.
If the display shows "CALC" together with a flashing drop symbol, both the descaling and the filter replacement are overdue.

## Energy saving

The machine enters standby after 25 minutes without use, and switches off completely after two hours. The standby delay can be changed to 10, 25 or 60 minutes in the settings menu under "ECO". Power consumption in standby is below 0.5 W.

## Warranty and service

The Crema Pro is covered by a three-year manufacturer's warranty, extended to five years if the product is registered at vellmar-service.example within 30 days of purchase. Defects caused by limescale, by the use of descalers other than DS-50, or by commercial use (more than 30 cups per day) are excluded. For repairs, call the Vellmar service line at 0800 471 220, Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00, and have the serial number ready; it is printed on the label under the drip tray and starts with CP22.
