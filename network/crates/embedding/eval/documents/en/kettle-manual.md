# QuickBoil kettle — user manual

Fictional product, written to measure the quality of the Silicium search. It looks like the coffee machine manual on purpose.

## Safety

Use the kettle only with the base supplied. Do not fill above the MAX mark, or boiling water may be ejected. Never open the lid while the water is boiling.

## Use

Fill the kettle with the amount of water needed, at least 0.5 litre, place it on the base and press the switch. The kettle switches off automatically when the water boils, after about three minutes for a full kettle.

## Descaling

Descale the kettle once a month in hard water areas, every three months otherwise. Fill it with a mix of half white vinegar and half water, let it stand for one hour without boiling, then rinse three times with clean water.

## Warranty

The kettle is guaranteed for one year. The warranty does not cover the filter, which is a wear part.
