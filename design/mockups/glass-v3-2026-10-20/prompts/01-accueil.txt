LATEST USER DIRECTION, PRIORITY: previous concepts were much too visually busy. This revision must be dramatically quieter, more spacious, and more readable while retaining subtle glass/gloss/relief. Do not repeat the heavy all-over blue glass aesthetic.
Art direction: 90–95% quiet pearl-white/off-white background (#f7f9fc), deep navy typography, tiny restrained blue accents. Almost no visible background texture; at most one very pale soft light field behind the hero. Frosted glass used only for navigation, one featured functional surface, and selected controls. Fine pale borders, soft short shadows, a slim top highlight; primary blue buttons have gentle convex relief. Other content sits directly on the page with generous whitespace and thin separators. Typography: crisp Geist-like sans serif, dark navy titles, comfortable secondary text; restrained scale, 16px body minimum, strong hierarchy. Desktop section gaps approximately 96px and clear 24–32px internal spacing. Keep the material tactile at close inspection without competing with content.
Brand reference: the supplied image is the NEW authentic Silicium logo from Downloads. It is a pale ice-blue glass diamond with raised 'Si' and layered rounded square backing. Preserve this exact image and its orientation, material and lettering, do not redesign it. Use it as a small 40px brand mark beside a dark navy 'Silicium' wordmark. White logo background blends with the quiet page.
